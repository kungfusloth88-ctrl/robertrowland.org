cube inside the sphere

A note on Trevor Paglen, Palladium #4, EW balloons, and UAP-shaped military weirdness.

why I looked at this

I was listening to a UFO podcast when I remembered Trevor Paglen’s Palladium #4.

The piece includes or references electronic-warfare balloon designs, one of which looks weirdly close to a description that has circulated in pilot and UAP accounts: a square or cube-like structure inside a transparent or spherical outer body.

That resemblance is what made the topic interesting. Not because it proves the sightings were balloons, and not because every strange aerial report collapses into a military-decoy explanation. The interesting part is narrower: some historical military deception systems look stranger than the public imagination usually allows.

short version

The “cube inside a sphere” description may sound exotic, but certain balloon-based radar or electronic-warfare designs can produce visually strange geometries: translucent outer envelopes, internal reflectors, suspended structures, and radar-enhancing shapes.

The more interesting question is not “was this specific UAP a balloon?” It is whether some sightings sit at the intersection of real physical objects, sensor deception, classification gaps, and cultural interpretation.

key distinctions

  • visible object vs. sensor target
