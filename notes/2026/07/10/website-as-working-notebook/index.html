website as working notebook

I do not know yet what this site is supposed to be.

That may be the point. The first mistake would be treating it like a portfolio, a résumé, or a polished blog. Those forms assume the thing already exists. This is closer to a notebook.

The site should keep track of questions, fragments, drafts, research trails, half-formed arguments, and the occasional finished piece when one survives long enough to become that.

The archive matters more than the homepage.
